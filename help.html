      *Member/Client Issues*


   The caller doesn't have/won't give a move number - how do I find it?

  1. Ask for the pickup or drop off zip code and search the moves in all status (NAVW, SOP, Hold, Delivered, Dry Run) and for all departments (ERS & Specialty) to see if you can pull it up.
  2. Confirm the date of the move. If the move is old enough - it will be older than the default filter for move date. Adjust the date - one day prior to the service date - in order to populate the move.
  3. Ask for the pickup State - if we had the zip code wrong in the system, you may be unable to find it using the zip code. Use the state and move date to try and pinpoint the move.
  4. Ask if the unit is going to Copart - or if it is a total loss. This will generally mean this call is for secondary - and will not populate in our system.
  5. Confirm if this a LD unit - sometimes they will be trying to reach NGIC / Insurance directly and end up transferred to us mistakenly. We would be unable to update them on the status if that is the case.

   The caller is stating they have an insurance not listed as a client. What do I do?

  1. Confirm what type of vehicle they have. If the unit is an LD unit - it is likely a subsidiary of NGIC and will need to reach back out to the main line for assistance and ensure they do not choose the "Recreational Vehicle" option which transfers them to TP.
  2. If the unit is an RV and the insurance company they have referenced is not listed as a client - advise that we can assist at an OOP cost and they can submit their invoice for reimbursement consideration if their insurance uses TP as a 3rd party vendor. Advise we are direct providers for NGIC for transparency. If they have a claim - gather the claim number and reach out to NGIC main line to see if they have record of the claim and confirm coverage.
  3. If the claim cannot be confirmed - and we still have not confirmed that the insurance is through NGIC - move forward at an OOP cost to the customer.


   A member/AAA is calling on a PO in Dry Run Status requesting service. What do I do?

  1. Advise this PO is in dry run status. Address the issue that caused the dry to ensure it has been fixed.
  2. Request a new PO for service. If the customer is calling direct - advise we will need a new work order from their roadside in order to assist once again.
  3. If there is any OOP collected on the OG move that is in dry run - a Team Lead must be notified in order to make adjustments necessary to moves. For clarity- the completed move must be the move we collected OOP on. If a new PO is issued and move is being redispatched - the OG move must be changed to a working status with the new PO and the new call in / dupe move must be assigned to OG carrier with the original dry run notes to process the GOA.
  4. Clearly notate both moves with a "Connected Move Note" - this should have both move #s and clearly state which move is the GOA PO and which move is the new PO to be worked.

   I received a roadside with a claim number (insurance) - but cannot confirm coverage because it is afterhours. What do I do?

  1. Ensure you have tried both the main line and email to confirm coverage and ensure everything is documented.
  2. Advise the member we are unable to confirm coverage afterhours and offer to have the unit towed to one of our provider's lots until coverage can be confirmed. If the member is planning on staying inside the unit and cannot be towed to storage - offer to have the unit towed to a nearby truck stop / rest area at an OOP cost. Advise that if coverage is confirmed in the morning - we can confirm with the client if we should reimburse out of pocket expenses or if they are to submit the invoice to the client directly for reimbursement consideration.
  3. If we have offered all options to get the unit off the road and the member does not want the unit towed until coverage is confirmed - address with your team lead / on call to confirm you have approval to leave the unit in its current spot until the morning. No moves should be left roadside overnight without a TEAM LEAD approval - regardless if the customer is requesting to wait.
  4. Keep the client updated throughout the process even if afterhours. If the member is refusing a tow until coverage is confirmed - we must advise client that we offered a tow to a truck stop and to our vendor lot for storage and the member denied both until coverage is confirmed.
  5. Notate the move with approvals and every step along the way.


      *Vendor Issues*


   The Vendor is on scene and saying it's going to cost more. What do I do?

  1. Get clarity on extra cost. If a destination changed - confirm the additional mileage lines up with TPG/Current Cost over TPG - and present to your Team Lead for approval. Ie: Vendor is currently assigned for a cost $100 over TPG. The new rate/TPG currently is $120 over the new TPG. This falls in line with original cost per mile - and will just need a team lead to approve in order to collect any additional funds from CX.
  2. Ensure to request photos of anything that will cause additional funds.
 Example 1: Unit is not accessible and requires winching - request photos of position and road leading to unit in order to address discrepancies in VCR and additional funds required.
 Example 2: The driveshaft bolts are stripped and cannot be removed - unit must be hauled or towed from the rear - Request photos of the driveshaft condition to present to CX/client if needed for GOA.
 Example 3: The vendor arrived on scene and is stating the unit is larger than described. Took out a MD and cannot tow the unit - Request photos on scene showing their equipment for GOA and gather new quote for HD to collect additional funds.
  3. After you have gathered all the pertinent facts - address with Team Lead for approval to present additional funds / request GOA as needed from client. Please ensure you are presenting SOLUTIONS - and not just problems for approval.


   The Vendor is delayed for pickup ETA. What do I do?

  1. Confirm in email chain / move notes if the move has a deadline for pickup or is urgent. If the client is requesting an urgent service and the ETA will no longer align with the client request - move on to the next available option and confirm ETA / negotiate cost as best as possible. Present this to your Team Lead to confirm if they would like to have this reassigned to another provider or update client on delayed ETA.
  2. If the unit has no clear pickup deadline - confirm with Client/CX if the delay is acceptable or if they would like for us to work on alternative options.
  3. Clearly note every phone call / approval to ensure there is a clear explanation on delay, request and approval. Ensure you notate any conversations with customer that result in additional funds being collected, or rescheduling of requests due to delays.
  4. Helios/Crane units - if the vendor is a CDC carrier scheduled to pickup ANYWHERE that does not have an afterhour pickup available - unit must be recovered and stored for our CDC carrier to pickup in order to not miss any deadlines we have scheduled. Examples:
 Helios - Crown point (Call Ambassador Auto - recover and store)
 Crane - Mt Prospect (Call RG Express - recover and store)
 Helios - Waller (Call Reflections Paint and Body - recover and store).
Ensure to let your Team Lead / Senior Dispatcher so the pickup address is updated on Central Dispatch. Ensure to let the vendor know where the new pickup is for afterhours access. Ensure the move is properly notated for transparency.

   The vendor is stating a unit was delivered, but client/cx is claiming it was not. What do I do?

  1. Reach out to the delivery location if a business to confirm the unit was received.
  2. If unit was going to customer direct (residence) and cx is confirming unit is not on site - address with vendor. Request all photos they have of the delivery and confirm the delivery address is correct with both CX and vendor.
  3. If the unit was marked delivered - place in hold ASAP to ensure vendor is not paid until we confirm the service was completed. Alert a team lead ASAP.
  4. If the unit was being picked up from a business - confirm with POC that the unit was picked up to ensure vendor did not update us in error.
  5. Follow up accordingly for photos/confirmation and notate the move clearly until we have confirmation.


   The vendor assigned is no longer answering the phone. What do I do?

  1. Send an email to see if we can get an update through there.
  2. Check Google for any alternate contact numbers we may not have on file for vendor.
  3. Begin working alternative options - try the next lowest quote and confirm their current ETA.
  4. Advise your team lead of the steps taken - and how long the vendor has been unresponsive for instructions on how to move forward.
