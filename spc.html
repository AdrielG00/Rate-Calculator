   Specialty Overview:


Intake:

A request is sent via email with the general information needed to gather a quote. When building the intake - the following must be included in every intake.
*Step 2 - Special Instructions:
"*No payment will be issued without the following*
1-Photos of where the keys were left. If there are no keys with the unit - Transit Pros must be notified prior to pickup for authorization. A rekey fee of $150 will be deducted from total pay if this is not provided.
2-Photos of the driveshaft and all bolts on the floorboard(if driveshaft required removal).
3-A detailed condition report with supporting photos.
4-Signed or Stamped release document provided in dispatch email."

*A Copy of the Transit Pros Condition Report must be uploaded into Files: Misc.
*PO must contain the word "Quote-" prior to the Stock/FLD#.
*A VIN# must be present within the damage details or special instructions for the vendor.
*Step 2 must be filled using the details provided and details gathered using the VIN #
*All photos provided must be uploaded into the move.
*The original intake email must be pasted into the move notes under the header "Client Intake"


Once the move is built:

1- Change date filter on Master Move list to 2020 and fill in the pickup location ZIP code to search completed moves.
*If this route has been done by a vendor multiple times - call that vendor to confirm their rate would be the same and an ETA.

2- If no similar moves - look up the delivery zip code and search for moves with similar mileage or near the pickup area.
*If this delivery is being frequented a specific vendor - call that vendor to confirm their rate.

3- If you do not have an option to present a lead based on previous moves - filter the vendor list accordingly based on mileage and put the move out to bid for 3 hours.

Once a Vendor quote is approved by a lead:

1- Use the client quote template to provide a quote to the client.

"Hello,
Please see below and advise if approved:

***STOCK # / PO # / CLAIM #***: $__.__
Mileage: __
Equipment: __

Let us know if this is approved and we will plan accordingly."

2- Notate the vendor name, vendor quote and client quote using the specialty template.
3- Update the client quote in the details tab to match the quoted rate to client.
4- Change the move to "Hold" status and notate "Pending Approval" along with the vendor's name.

Once the client approves a quote:

1- Upload the PDF Release provided by the client in the approval email under Files - Misc.
2- Update the POC Name, Number, PL Location Name and any special instructions provided for contact.
3- Remove "Quote-" from the PO.
4- Copy the damage details/special instructions in the approval paperwork onto the damage details for Step 2.
5- Attempt to call the pickup location for a step 2. A complete step 2 should include the following:
   Is the unit released and ready for pickup? No balance due?
   Hours of Operation and any lead time needed for scheduling
   Are there keys with the unit?
   All damage details provided need confirmed
   Is the driveshaft connected?
   Is the unit blocked in or accessible for us to hook and go?
   Are the tires roadworthy? Holding air? No signs of dry rot or cracking?
6- If no answer - notate your attempt and for a next attempt to tried in 3 hours. If step is completed - reach out to the vendor to get the pickup scheduled. Relay all special instructions that were gathered during step 2.
7- If dispatching a vendor - the specialty disclaimer must be read verbally prior to sending any paperwork.    Specialty Disclaimer:

We are dispatching this tow for [QUOTE] all-inclusive with an ETA of [date with time ONLY if applicable] as discussed. Pre-Approval is required for all additional funds requests - If anything is other than described, additional services requested or any other changes that would affect cost, please call for prior approval.
*All GOA requests require notification while the driver is on scene along with photos of equipment on scene submitted to specialty@transitpros.com.
*No payment will be issued without the following 4 requirements*
1- Photos of where the keys were left. If there are no keys with the unit - Transit Pros must be notified prior to pickup for authorization. A rekey fee of $150 will be deducted from total pay if this is not provided.
2- Photos of the driveshaft and all bolts on the floorboard(if driveshaft required removal).
3- A detailed condition report with a minimum of 4 corner supporting photos.
4- Signed or Stamped release document provided in dispatch email."

Once a move is assigned:

1- CSR must attempt to schedule all appointments regardless of notifying carrier of special instructions - Pickup and Delivery must be called with an update. *It is the duty of the assigned agent to do everything possible to minimize the chances of a dry run.
2- Client must be updated with ETA.
3- Move must be properly notated with who the CSR spoke to at both locations,
4- Move must be properly notated with the next follow up - 24hrs prior to pickup to confirm on schedule.
